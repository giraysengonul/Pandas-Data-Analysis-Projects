# iPhone Sales Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing iPhone sales data from Flipkart (India) using Python.  
The main goal is to understand the relationship between iPhone prices, ratings, and reviews, and to identify top-performing and least-performing iPhone models based on user feedback.

The analysis was performed using **Pandas**, **NumPy**, and **Plotly** for interactive and insightful visualizations.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies & Libraries Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Plotly Express
- Plotly Graph Objects

---

## 📊 Key Analysis Questions & Insights

### 1️⃣ Top 10 Highest-Rated iPhones on Flipkart (India)
- Identified the top 10 iPhone models with the highest average ratings.
- This analysis helps highlight which models receive the most positive customer feedback.


---

### 2️⃣ Number of Ratings for the Highest-Rated iPhones
- Analyzed how many users rated the highest-rated iPhone models.
- This helps determine whether high ratings are supported by a large number of users or a limited sample.

📷 **Visualization:** `assets/Page1.png`

---

### 3️⃣ iPhone with the Highest Number of Reviews on Flipkart
- Determined which iPhone model has received the highest number of reviews.
- Indicates the most popular or most purchased iPhone model on the platform.

📷 **Visualization:** `assets/Page2.png`

---

### 4️⃣ Relationship Between Sale Price and Number of Ratings
- Explored how the sale price of iPhones relates to the number of ratings.
- Helps understand whether more affordable models receive more engagement or if premium models attract more attention.

📷 **Visualization:** `assets/Page3.png`

---

### 5️⃣ Relationship Between Sale Price and Number of Reviews
- Analyzed the correlation between iPhone prices and total review counts.
- Provides insights into customer behavior across different price segments.

📷 **Visualization:** `assets/Page4.png`

---

### 6️⃣ Most Expensive and Least Expensive iPhones
- Identified the highest-priced and lowest-priced iPhone models available on Flipkart.
- Useful for comparing pricing extremes and their corresponding popularity.

---

## 📈 Key Takeaways
- High ratings do not always correlate with a high number of reviews.
- Mid-range priced iPhones tend to receive higher engagement in terms of ratings and reviews.
- The most expensive iPhones are not always the most reviewed.
- Customer engagement is influenced by both price and perceived value.
